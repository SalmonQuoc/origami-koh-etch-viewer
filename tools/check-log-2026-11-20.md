The random-wafer test on the (100) lecture layout (500 wafers, δ uniform in ±5°) read the fan on 90 % of wafers with an rms error of 0.0007° before the width-reading limit was added. The arc read 47 % of the wafers, as expected for an arc that covers ±2°.

## Fixes made after the checks

1. **Flat lengths.** These came out at 32.498 mm because a clipped 360-gon cuts chords, not the circle. The outline is now built analytically with exact flat endpoints.
2. **Corner probe.** It read 200 µm at t = 0 because it walked into the open area. It now counts only masked nodes.
3. **Arc estimator.** It was outside its ± in about 8 % of cases, when the zero run touched an end of the arc. Those readings are now marked invalid.
4. **V-fit.** The fit was unrealistically precise. Widths are now read to 1 % (design choice, `FD.relW`).
5. **Corner readout.** For beams it said "used up before the first sample", because the beam remnant is not on the diagonal. It now reports when the mesa corner is first attacked.
6. **Layout.** The scene buttons and the finder type list overflowed at 390 px; both now wrap. The "flat direction" label overlapped the "true ⟨110⟩" label and was moved to the other end of the wafer.

## Known limits

- The finder etches each structure with the analytic plane model. Neighbouring cavities are overlaid, not merged.
- The reading model ignores edge roughness, rate scatter and mask-writer angle steps (Pal & Sato p. 304).
- The EDP rates outside {100}, {110} and {111} are interpolated, which is an assumption.
- The corner-lab beam lengths for KOH follow a 33 wt% rule. Values that are not given in the sources are design choices.
