## Pass 4. Convex-corner undercut on (100)

Measured: bevel α = 30 ± 1° to ⟨110⟩ in 35 wt% KOH at 76 °C (Pal & Sato p. 231; Fig. 6.21, p. 234 for 34 wt% at 70 °C),
corner 150 ± 2° (p. 235); [M] p. 3948: ⟨410⟩ trace of {411} at 30.96°, corner 151.9° (Fig. 2).

320 µm square mesa, 40 min, KOH at 70 °C (34 wt% = linear between the 30 and 40 wt% columns, assumption):

| KOH | simulator α (dx 2) | rate-map α (minimum envelope, p. 228) | l/d (dx 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 wt% | 23.6° | 25.0° | 3.51 |

| 40 wt% | 25.7° | 26.3° | 3.24 |
| 50 wt% | 27.3° | 28.4° | 2.28 |

Result: the simulator follows the bevel of its own rate map within 1.5°; the map gives about 5° less than measured.
Cause: Table 4.1 has no orientation between (100) and (310)/(311), so the {411} planes that bound the corner in KOH ([M])
get interpolated rates. Not changed: no sourced {411} rate is available (the [M] ratio R(411)/R(100) is given only as a plotted curve, Fig. 4).
The undercut length is not grid-converged: l = 78, 97, 109 µm at dx 4, 2, 1 (34 wt%).
Change: new case in `tools/accuracy.mjs`, new test (simulated α within 2.5° of the rate-map α), the limit stated on the
page (How it works part 6, part 10; simulator "Model, sources and assumptions") and in the README.

## Pass 5. Sideways {100} undercut lag (open item of 2026-11-22)

100 µm window turned 45° on (100), 30 wt% KOH; sub-cell zero crossing of φ along the diagonal:

| dx | t | undercut at 3 / 5 / 11 µm depth | exact R(100)·t | lag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 µm | 20 min | 11.95 / 11.94 / 11.55 µm | 15.94 µm | 4.0 µm |
| 2 µm | 40 min | 27.92 / 27.92 / 27.92 µm | 31.88 µm | 4.0 µm |
| 2 µm | 60 min | 43.77 / 43.60 / 40.17 µm | 47.82 µm | 4.0 µm |
| 1 µm | 40 min | 29.65 / 29.65 / 29.65 µm | 31.88 µm | 2.2 µm |

Diagnosis: the lag is a constant offset of about two cells that builds up in the first minutes (at t = 4 min the top row
under the mask has moved 0.2 µm against 3.2 µm) and does not grow afterwards; it falls about linearly with dx. The wall
then moves at R(100). It comes from the Lax-Friedrichs treatment of the masked top row while the front there is still tilted.
Tried and dropped: treating every masked top-row node with a downward-rising φ as a vertical wall (pz = 0): the
45° undercut improves by 0.8 µm, but a ⟨110⟩ pit is undercut by 58 µm and a 10 µm ⟨110⟩ Si wall disappears, because the
{111} wall then moves sideways at R(110). Change: the limit is now stated with these numbers (page, README); new dev tool
`tools/undercut-lag.mjs`.
